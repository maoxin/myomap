MyoMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. We do not collect any personal data

MyoMap (“the app”) requires no account or sign-in and contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking. It does not send any of your information to us or to any third-party server, and it works without an internet connection.

2. Data stored on your device

To provide its features, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • symptom descriptions you enter (search history, up to 30 entries);
  • the body areas from your most recent check (used to tailor training plans); pain areas you draw are kept in memory only while the app is open;
  • pain scores and notes in your pain log;
  • your saved training plans and daily check-ins;
  • your daily reminder setting and time, and your language preference.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time in About → Erase all data on this device; uninstalling the app also deletes it.

3. In-app purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether a purchase or trial is active on your account.

4. Notifications

The app asks for notification permission only if you turn on the daily training reminder, and reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

5. Health information

Content in the app is for general health education and self-care only. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, please consult a qualified health professional.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: maoxin.jz@gmail.com

肌识 MyoMap 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

一、我们不收集任何个人数据

肌识（以下简称“本应用”）不需要注册或登录，不包含任何广告、统计分析或第三方追踪代码，也不会把你的任何信息发送到我们或任何第三方的服务器。本应用运行时不需要联网。

二、保存在你设备上的数据

为了提供功能，以下内容仅保存在你的设备本地：

  • 你输入的症状描述（搜索记录，最多 30 条）；
  • 最近一次分析出的疼痛部位（用于训练计划）；你在身上画出的区域只在当前使用中保留；
  • 疼痛日记中的疼痛评分与备注；
  • 你保存的训练计划与每日打卡记录；
  • 每日训练提醒的开关与时间，以及语言偏好。

这些数据不会离开你的设备。你可以在应用内“关于 → 清除本机全部数据”随时删除；卸载应用也会一并删除。

三、应用内购买

免费试用和一次性解锁完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会获得你的姓名、支付信息或 Apple ID，本应用只会向 App Store 查询你的账户是否已开始试用或已购买。

四、通知

只有在你主动开启“每日训练提醒”时，本应用才会请求通知权限，并在设备本地安排提醒。你可以随时在应用内或系统“设置”中关闭。

五、健康信息声明

本应用提供的内容仅用于健康科普与自我保健参考，不是医疗器械，不构成医疗诊断或治疗建议。如有不适，请咨询专业医生。

六、儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

七、政策变更与联系方式

如本政策发生变化，我们会在此页面更新。如有疑问，请联系：maoxin.jz@gmail.com
