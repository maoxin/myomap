FAQ

How much does it cost? MyoMap is free to download with a 7-day free trial of every feature. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks training plans, the pain log and 3D exercise demos forever. There is no subscription and you are never charged automatically. Pain Finder, emergency alerts and the Atlas are always free.

I reinstalled the app or switched phones. Open About (ⓘ) → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I request a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice? No. MyoMap is for general health education and self-care and does not diagnose. If your pain is severe, getting worse, or comes with numbness, weakness, fever or chest pain, please see a doctor or call emergency services.
