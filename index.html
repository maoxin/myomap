MyoMap: 3D Muscle Pain Finder

Describe where it hurts, or draw it on the body, and see the muscles most likely involved on a real 3D anatomy model, with stretches, strengthening exercises, training plans and a pain log. Works fully offline, no account needed.

How it works

  1. Type a sentence such as “stiff neck when I woke up, right side hurts”, or tap Draw where it hurts and shade the area with your finger.
  2. The muscles that fit best light up on the 3D body, each with the words or area it was matched from.
  3. Open a muscle for its typical pain pattern, a self-check and exercises with 3D demos.

Results are inferred on your device from the words you use and the areas you draw. They are for reference only and are not a diagnosis. If something sounds urgent, such as chest pain spreading to the arm, the app tells you to get medical help first.

肌识：3D 肌肉疼痛地图

用一句话描述哪里不舒服，或直接在身上画出来，就能在真实的 3D 解剖模型上看到最可能相关的肌肉，并获得拉伸、强化动作、训练计划和疼痛日记。完全离线，无需注册。

怎么用

  1. 输入一句话，比如“早上起床落枕了，脖子右边疼”；或者点画出疼痛位置，用手指在人体上涂出疼的地方。
  2. 最相关的肌肉会在 3D 人体上亮起来，并写明是根据你的哪些话、哪块区域推测的。
  3. 点开肌肉，查看典型疼痛表现、自我检测，以及带 3D 演示的放松和强化动作。

结果在你的手机上根据你描述的词语和画出的部位推测，仅供参考，不是诊断。遇到可能危急的情况（比如胸痛放射到手臂），应用会先提示你就医。
